### Product-type photographic editor previews (2026-11-20)

They depict representative garment cuts, drinkware, bags, stationery, caps and gifts; they are illustrative previews rather than PHULO inventory photographs. Front/back crew-neck, long-sleeve, polo and hoodie assets are separate. No VistaPrint design files, logos, or product photos were copied into these assets.

VistaPrint's public T-shirt, full-sleeve, tote-bag and personalised-mug product/gallery pages were used as references for category navigation, garment construction, template selection and editable print placement. `mockupSurfaces.ts` selects the product-type asset and printable area; `apparelDesigns.ts` supplies original editable sample artwork. Artwork remains separate from the product photograph, and texture shading is a non-editable preview layer. Actual product dimensions, materials and printing specifications still require PHULO's quote confirmation.
