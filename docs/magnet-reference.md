# Photo magnet flow reference

Reviewed public VistaPrint India pages on 2026-11-23:

- https://www.vistaprint.in/photo-gifts/magnets/extra-large-photo
- https://www.vistaprint.in/photo-gifts/magnets/extra-large-photo/templates
- https://www.vistaprint.in/photo-gifts/magnets/fridge-magnets
- https://www.vistaprint.in/photo-gifts/acrylic-fridge-magnets

The reference flow starts with product information and quantity/options, then offers Browse designs or Upload design. The gallery has search, photo-count/style/colour filters and favourites, then opens the selected template for editing and review. Reference acrylic magnets include circle, heart, rounded square and starburst designs. Reference product dimensions/prices are manufacturer-specific; they are not substituted for PHULO's catalogue data.

PHULO now starts photo/fridge/acrylic magnets at product specifications. Existing size, orientation and shape selections persist through template selection, the studio and the approved order. Quantity persists into the order request. Circle and custom-cut previews use square artwork. Heart and starburst are previews under the existing **Custom shape** quote option; the studio saves the exact cut, while final dimensions, cut and pricing require PHULO confirmation. No product price or manufacturing specification is invented.

The gallery renders original editable designs from the same layout function used by the studio, clipped to the selected magnet cut. There are no people or refrigerator scenes in template thumbnails. Template switching preserves dimensions, cut and owned uploaded photos rather than forcing landscape. Original AI-generated single-magnet catalogue photos replace only shipped placeholders/reference photos; administrator-uploaded images remain unchanged. Customer photos remain protected uploads and are rendered into the preview and saved order design.

Verification includes real temporary MongoDB/API registration and login, owned photo upload, text editing, review approval and order creation with quantity three and a starburst cut. Additional native-canvas tests verify custom-cut clipping and preservation through option/layout changes. Production data is not used for these test writes.
